The process is the proof
The looser, more human cut. Scanned paper, hand annotations over photos, contact sheets, test strips, Polaroids. Pale blue + stone + ivory + ink. Anton + Chivo + Caveat for a single hand-written line of marginalia. Reads as working, open, curious.
01 / brand board · 02 / imagery rule · 03 / section mark · 04 / do and don't

Do

  • Use Caveat only for marginalia, one line maximum.
  • Let paper texture show through.
  • Date everything.
  • Pale blue is the paper tape, nothing more.

Don't

  • Never use Caveat for the headline.
  • Never over-handwrite.
  • Never clean out imperfections.
