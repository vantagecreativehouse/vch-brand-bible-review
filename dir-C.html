Earned by doing, documented
For legacy manufacturing clients and their peers. Stone grounds, metal, dust, wood. Anton + JetBrains Mono used as a stamped-receipt second voice for dates and serials. Safety-orange appears only on the physical world (cone, hard-hat band), never on type. Reads as hard-won, documentary, specific.
01 / brand board · 02 / imagery rule · 03 / section mark · 04 / do and don't

Do

  • Date-stamp every piece in mono.
  • Treat mono as a receipt, not decoration.
  • Include one physical artefact per frame.
  • Let the floor be a floor.

Don't

  • Never clean the floor.
  • Never set data in Chivo.
  • Never put the accent on type.
