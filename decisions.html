04 / Decisions

Three decisions for the morning

The smallest set of answers we need to carry the brand into real pieces.

01. Which direction.

one, or a hybrid of two
A, B, C, D or E. Hybrid is fine, say which two and which is the body — for example "B lead, A for the longer essays."
A   Earned Editorial
We have the opinion we earned over 30 years
B   Light and Silence
We see what matters
C   Industrial Testimony
Earned by doing, documented
D   Studio Notebook
The process is the proof
E   Signal Over Noise
We do not need to shout

02. The logo lockup.

do we draft or do you provide
Right now the system uses a square section mark + "Vantage Creative House" set in Anton. No standalone mark. If we are drafting a lockup, we propose 3 options tomorrow night (a square mark + wordmark, a monogram, a seal). If you have a lockup already, drop it into 00_Inbox and we build around it.

03. First piece to publish.

once a direction is locked
We think it is a short LinkedIn post about the thesis of VCH ("earned expertise, well-recorded") landed in the picked direction. One statement, one piece of proof. Agree, or name a different first piece and we start there.
