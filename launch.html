Lead vs runner-up

Eight launch pieces for the VCH feed, same statements, two directions. We picked B. You answer with a letter.

Why B wins.

The three lines.
01. The home base of your eye is light with an opinion, built in camera, not graded in post. B puts that at the centre of the brand instead of behind it.
02. The peer-set research found one open lane: nobody owns AI as a brand aesthetic. We cannot own AI, but we can own light, which is the thing AI slop still cannot fake.
03. B keeps the burnt orange honest: the practical lamp, nothing else, so the only saturated colour in VCH is always a thing you can point to in the room.
