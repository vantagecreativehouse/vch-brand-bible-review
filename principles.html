01 / Principles

The things that are true
in every direction

These do not change between A and E. The directions are five answers to the same question.

Voice.

voice.md
We. Not I.
Quietly confident.
Short lines, one thought per line.
No em dashes between words.
No exclamation marks.
No corporate speak.
AI enhances. It does not replace.
The gate — before anything leaves the folder.
  1. Does this reinforce that earned expertise deserves earned recognition?
  2. Would a smart warm friend who wants you to do well say this?
  3. Could anyone have written this? If yes, rewrite until only we could have.

Erin's taste.

taste.md
His signature, in his words:
"The thing that makes my work mine is my use of light, texture, and constant flowing movement."

Home base: darker, edgier, rich and warm but also cool and dark. Natural teal-and-orange built in camera, not in post.
North stars
  • Greig Fraser — texture, rich palette, monumental low-key.
  • Roger Deakins — monochromatic schemes layered with light and shadow.
  • Chivo Lubezki — reds, blues and skin tones together.
  • Marcell Rév — colour creating depth, movement creating surprise.
  • Caravaggio — one source, deep shadow, drama.
The anti
Flat, opinion-less light. Light must have an opinion.

The AI slop test.

applies to every generated image
Texture. If the frame does not have skin texture, fabric weave, metal patina, paper fibre or hair edge, it does not leave the folder. Real light gives things texture. If it does not, it is slop.

Must not look like.

the universal avoid-list
AI-slop IG.
Plastic skin, floating hands, perfect symmetry, no texture, no shadow with weight.
Agency gradient-mesh.
Big heavy sans-serif on a purple-pink gradient. "Transforming narratives." Not us.
Hype startup aesthetic.
All caps. All emojis. Growth-hack voice. We are the opposite of this.
Canva-template energy.
Stock photos, round-cornered shapes, free-font pairings, pastel backgrounds.
Crypto-maximalist.
Pure black, neon green, pixel noise. Not legacy. Not us.
Studio-for-studios.
Portfolio-site minimalism so refined it says nothing. The failure mode of good taste.
