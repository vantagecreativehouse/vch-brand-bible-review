We have the opinion we earned over 30 years
Documentary classic. Ivory ground, Anton statements with a trailing period, Chivo body. One section colour per screen. One burnt-orange accent per piece. Reads as considered, grown-up, confident.
01 / brand board · 02 / imagery rule · 03 / section mark · 04 / do and don't

Do

  • One trailing period on every Anton statement.
  • One section colour per screen.
  • Caption lives below the image, never over it.
  • One burnt-orange accent per piece, never two.

Don't

  • Never set Anton smaller than 72px.
  • Never over-grade the images.
  • Never let the accent compete with ink.
